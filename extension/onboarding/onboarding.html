Welcome to Define & Translate

AI-powered contextual definitions and translations for any text you select on the web.

Before you get started

To provide the service, this extension collects the following data. Please review it before continuing.

Usage Information

The text you select and its surrounding text (together up to 300 characters) is sent to a third-party AI service to generate the definition and translation. It is your responsibility to ensure no personal or confidential information is selected or in the surrounding text. The website address (excluding any query strings or personal identifiers) is used to validate that the Extension is operating on an appropriate page. This information is not stored by us once your lookup request is completed. Language preference and user consent used to provide the service are stored in your Extension's local storage.

Personal Information

An anonymous installation identifier from your Extension is used for usage analytics and troubleshooting, combined with your IP address for rate-limiting. No raw IP address is stored. This information is stored only for a limited period.

Generated Content

Successful AI responses (definition and translation) are briefly cached to improve service performance.

By clicking Get Started, you agree to this data collection as described in our Privacy Policy.

Sorry to see you go

The extension will not collect any data. To remove it from Chrome, please follow one of the steps below.

1

Right-click the Define & Translate icon in the Chrome toolbar, then select "Remove from Chrome"

or

2

Go to chrome://extensions, find Define & Translate, and click "Remove"
